Wab Fit support

For questions, feedback and faults: support@wabfit.com. Replies within two working days.

Before writing

Please state the device and its operating system, the version shown under Profile → About, and a description of what happened and what was expected.

Deleting the account

The account is deleted from inside the app, immediately and permanently. The steps are here.

Privacy and terms

The privacy policy sets out what is collected, what is not, and why. The terms of use set out what is allowed between friends and what is not.

Not a medical device

Wab Fit records what the user enters and displays what their devices send. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or any dose or medication recommendation. Treatment decisions should be taken with a qualified professional.
